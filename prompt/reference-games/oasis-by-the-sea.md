How the person played, as they describe it:

- Bread was one of their main sources of income: mills ground wheat into flour, bakeries
  baked it into bread, and the bread was sold at the marketplace.
- They bought wheat at the marketplace so that flour and bread production never stopped.
- A church and a cathedral raised popularity.
- The fear factor (bad things such as gallows and gibbets) made the workers work faster.

Their settlement at the end, read from the game:

- 202 people in 24 hovels, with no free housing; popularity 100.
- 57 bakeries, 7 mills and 10 wheat farms. The marketplace sold bread automatically.
- 13 iron mines and 10 quarries with 33 ox tethers, and 10 woodcutters.
- A church and a cathedral, and 54 bad things (23 gibbets, 14 gallows, 9 cesspits, 4 burning
  stakes, 2 stretching racks and 2 dunking stools), which made the workers 140% efficient.
- No inns and no good things. No taxes, and full rations of bread. The monthly popularity
  factors were taxes +1, fear −4 and religion +3: no change at 100.
