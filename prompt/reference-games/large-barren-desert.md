How the person played, as they describe it:

- Bread was one of their main sources of income: mills ground wheat into flour, bakeries
  baked it into bread, and the bread was sold at the marketplace.
- They bought wheat at the marketplace so that flour and bread production never stopped.
- A church and a cathedral raised popularity, and so did inns.
- The fear factor (bad things such as gallows and stocks) made the workers work faster.

Their settlement at the end, read from the game:

- 250 people in 30 hovels, with no free housing; popularity 100.
- 95 bakeries and 12 mills, and no wheat farms.
- 11 inns, which reached everyone with ale, and no brewery or hops farm.
- 13 quarries and 7 iron mines with 37 ox tethers; no woodcutters.
- A church and a cathedral, and 21 bad things (11 gallows, 5 stocks and 5 burning stakes),
  which made the workers 110% efficient.
- Low taxes and no rations. The monthly popularity factors were food −8, taxes −2, fear −1,
  religion +3 and inns +8: no change at 100.
