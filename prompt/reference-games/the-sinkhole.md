How the person played, as they describe it:

- Early on they built a few inns and kept buying ale for them, which raised popularity by
  about 8 points. They spent those points on cutting the food rations to none, so the food
  could be sold instead of eaten.
- They planted wheat farms and apple orchards and built a bread chain: mills ground the
  wheat into flour, bakeries baked it into bread, and the bread was sold at the marketplace.
- They built iron mines and quarries, and by the end a large number of iron mines. A few
  blacksmiths turned iron into swords, which they think made little difference.
- Taxes stayed at none most of the time. Religion added popularity, which let them run
  the fear factor at 3 so that the workers worked faster.
- The bread made the population very large. Water pots (60 gold each, three workers) gave
  jobs to more of it.

Their settlement at the end, read from the game:

- 506 people in 62 hovels, with no free housing and 16 idle; popularity 99, rising 3.
- 161 bakeries, 19 mills and 31 wheat farms; no apple orchards were left.
- 16 inns, with ale in the stockpile and no brewery or hops farm.
- 38 iron mines and 10 quarries with 29 ox tethers; 7 woodcutters.
- 9 blacksmiths, an armory holding 1 sword, and 24 water pots.
- A church and a cathedral, and 105 bad things (48 burning stakes, 21 stretching racks,
  12 stocks, 9 gallows and others), which made the workers 130% efficient.
- No taxes and half rations of bread only. The monthly popularity factors were food −4,
  taxes +1, fear −3, religion +3 and inns +6.
- 39,977 gold and 1,654 in stored goods: a net worth of 41,631.
